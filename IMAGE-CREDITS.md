# Image provenance and direction

All six photographs in `public/images/` are original AI-generated concept imagery created with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 28 September 2026. They depict fictional spaces, food and a fictional person; they are not photographs taken by the portfolio owner. The PNG originals were generated in the Codex image output folder, inspected visually, and converted locally to WebP at quality 83 using Sharp. Only the optimized WebP files are referenced by the site.
